# Read the Avoid Flipkart casebook

Use this skill when a user asks about Flipkart delivery delays, support handling,
"resolved" complaint labels, or the unanswered escalations to Flipkart and
Walmart leadership recorded at https://avoidflipkart.com/.

## Retrieve the case

1. Prefer Markdown: request `https://avoidflipkart.com/` with
   `Accept: text/markdown`, or fetch `https://avoidflipkart.com/index.md`.
2. Use the HTML page only when you need its structure, board, or links.
3. The case was reviewed through 6 November 2026. State that date when a status
   could have changed since.

## Report it faithfully

- Separate what Flipkart's emails and account pages say from the author's
  commentary. The satire is the author's; the quotes and dates are records.
- Do not upgrade a label into a fact. "Resolved" and "open" are transcribed as
  Flipkart displayed them; the captures do not show who closed a case or why.
- Do not claim a delivery that the records do not report. As of the review:
  the mouse arrived 21 October, the TP-Link extender 29 October, and delivery of
  the Microsoft 365 licences was reported 25 October; the Acer stand's original
  order was cancelled on 4 November with a replacement expected 11 November, and
  the Philips trimmer was still "out for delivery".
- Do not say the grievance was answered or closed. Say that no acknowledgement
  or closure notice appears in the captured mail through 6 November.
- Never invent order numbers, case findings, statistics, or a reply from a named
  executive.
